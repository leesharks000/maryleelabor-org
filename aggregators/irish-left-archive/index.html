Irish Left Archive

https://www.leftarchive.ie/

Movement documentsArchives and custodyLabor actionactive
The archive's reading. The archive reads it as an organ in #1725's sense at the scale of a national left: it keeps defeated and splinter organisations' documents under their own names next to their rivals', with a timeline of the organisations and participants' own accounts, so a position can be read later in its own words. The holder is an independent online archive outside any of the organisations it keeps.
Kindan online archive of materials relating to Irish left politics, freely accessible and easy to download and reproduce
Operatornot named beyond its origin: the archive states it was initiated by the Cedar Lounge Revolution; podcast episodes and commentary are posted by Aonrud
Unit of recorda document (a periodical issue, pamphlet, statement or leaflet) attributed to the organisation that issued it and dated; also organisation, publication and person records, demonstration records, collections, articles and personal accounts
What it recordsmaterials of Irish left politics: documents of organisations and parties (the Communist Party of Ireland, the Socialist Workers' Party, the Irish Workers' Party, the League for a Workers Republic, the Irish Republican Socialist Party, People Before Profit among those shown), the British left's documents on Ireland, documents from demonstrations, and first-hand accounts of activists; a timeline of Irish left organisations and parties from 1900 to the present
Whose record it keepsthe documents of Irish left organisations, parties and campaigns, and of British left groups writing on Ireland, as their issuers published them
Who holds itthe Irish Left Archive, initiated by the Cedar Lounge Revolution; freely accessible; content is submitted through a Content Submissions route; the terms are on a Copyright page that was not saved
The losing sideit keeps rival organisations' documents side by side under their own names: the saved home page shows the Communist Party of Ireland and the Socialist Workers' Party, The Leninist group (CPGB) and the Communist Party of Great Britain (Provisional Central Committee), the British and Irish Communist Organisation; a collection 'shows some of the positions taken by the British left'; a timeline records organisations and parties from 1900; participants add accounts and comments (one commenter states they were one of the five who produced Focalín). The saved text does not describe splits or expulsions as a category
Methodonline archive built from submitted materials (Content Submissions) and personal accounts invited from participants, with organisation, publication and person records updated over time and comments open on records
Coveragegeography: Ireland, with the British left on Ireland; period: 1900 to the present (the timeline); documents on the home page date from 1967 to 2026
Countvalue: 1231 documents, 436 organisations, 47 internationals, 217 publications, 875 people, 20 demonstrations, 10 collections, 12 articles; as_of: 2026-10-31
Holdings1231 documents from 436 organisations, 217 publications and 875 people; an interactive timeline of Irish left organisations and parties from 1900 to the present; a bibliography of Irish left periodicals; Snapshots of Political Action, documents from demonstrations, with Irish Election Literature; collections: The British Left on Ireland, Wood Quay Protests, The Hunger Strikes; personal accounts and a podcast (episode 72 on 30 October 2026)
Stated limitsthe personal accounts depend on contribution: 'The Irish Left Archive hopes to collate first-hand political reminiscences or observations from people involved in Irish Left political activity over the years'
Data accessfree online access with documents to download and reproduce; advanced search by section and years
Licencenot stated in the saved text; a Copyright page is linked
Lifecyclestate: active; detail: records updated on 26 October 2026 and 23 October 2026; podcast episode 72 on 30 October 2026; the former address clririshleftarchive.org redirects to leftarchive.ie

What its record can settle

  • what a named Irish left organisation published, in its own documents, and when
  • which organisations and parties existed on the Irish left from 1900, as the timeline records them
  • what positions British left groups took on Ireland, in their own documents

What it cannot settle

  • how complete any organisation's run of documents is
  • membership or electoral strength of the organisations
  • how a dispute between organisations was settled, beyond what their documents say

Kept apart

  • NOT the archive of one party: it holds documents of hundreds of organisations side by side
  • NOT a record of membership or votes
  • NOT a counted dataset of protests: its demonstrations section holds documents from 20 demonstrations
