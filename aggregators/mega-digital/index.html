Texts of the traditionactive
The archive's reading. #1725 places Marx's practice turned on his own programme and texts on the side of sublation; the archive reads a complete edition of drafts, excerpts and letters as the record of that self-revision, kept with the stages the published text superseded. The page quotes Die Zeit (1999) on the edition's beginning, failure and resurrection and on opening the work 'originalgetreu, das heißt unzensiert', so the edition's own history is a case of a holder changing after 1989.

What its record can settle

  • what Marx and Engels wrote, drafted, excerpted and received, in an edited text faithful to the originals
  • where a passage of Das Kapital or its preparatory works stands in the edited text of Abteilung II
  • which MEGA volumes have appeared, in print or online, and when

What it cannot settle

  • the reception of Marx and Engels in later movements and parties
  • the writings of other socialists except as correspondents in letters to Marx and Engels
  • the full text of volumes published only in print (from the saved sources)

Kept apart

  • NOT a library of the Marxist tradition: it edits Marx and Engels only
  • NOT complete online: Abteilung I and part of the edition appear in print
  • NOT a reading edition or translation: a historical-critical edition
