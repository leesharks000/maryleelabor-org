What its record can settle

  • whether a named union or labor organization's records are held, through the finding aids
  • what the library holds for a union in its browse categories (AFSCME, AFT, IWW, UAW, UFW and the rest)

What it cannot settle

  • the size of the holdings, from the saved text
  • whether the collections keep the records of union opposition caucuses, dissent or expulsions, from the saved text

Kept apart

  • NOT the UAW's own archive: a Wayne State University library, though the UAW funded its building
  • NOT a strike dataset: its podcast and subject headings discuss strikes, and its unit is the archival collection
  • NOT limited to labor: it also holds the Wayne State University Archives and Detroit urban affairs
