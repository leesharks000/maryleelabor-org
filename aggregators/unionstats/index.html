Union Membership and Coverage Database

https://www.unionstats.com/

Worker poweractive
The archive's reading. none stated. The archive reads the database as the measure of the base a labor organization stands on, held by independent academics outside any union; it counts members and keeps none of their positions.
Kindan Internet data resource of union membership, coverage and density estimates, published as historical tables
OperatorBarry Hirsch (Andrew Young School of Policy Studies, Georgia State University), David Macpherson (Department of Economics, Trinity University) and William Even (Department of Economics, Miami University)
Unit of recordan estimate of union membership, coverage, density and employment (and union and nonunion wages) for a cell: a sector by demographic group, state, metropolitan area, industry or occupation, by year
What it recordsprivate and public sector labor union membership, coverage and density in the United States, and union wages, nonunion wages and union wage gaps, compiled from the monthly household Current Population Survey
Whose record it keepsthe measured extent of union membership and coverage among wage and salary workers surveyed in the CPS
Who holds itthree academic economists, who compile and update the tables annually; use of the data requires citation
The losing sidenot recorded
Methodsurvey microdata: estimates compiled from the monthly household Current Population Survey (CPS) using BLS methods
Coveragegeography: United States: economy-wide, by state, by metropolitan area, by industry and occupation; period: economy-wide from 1973; state, industry and occupation from 1983; metropolitan area from 1986; through 2025
Countvalue: not stated as a count of tables or estimates; as_of: compilations from the 2025 CPS completed 2026-01-23
Holdingshistorical tables of union membership, density and employment by sector and demographic group (1973-2025), by sector and state (1983-2025), by sector and metropolitan area (1986-2025), by industry (1983-2025) and by occupation (1983-2025); historical tables of union wages, nonunion wages and union wage gaps by sector and demographic group (headed 1973-2022, its table listed as 1973-2025); CPS information and supporting documentation
Stated limits'CPS sample sizes are very small for some cells. Use such estimates cautiously.'
Data accesshistorical tables on unionstats.com; use of data requires citation (Hirsch, Macpherson and Even 2026); email for support
Licencenot stated as a licence; '© 2026. Use of data requires citation.'
Lifecyclestate: active; detail: created in 2002 and updated annually; compilations from the 2025 CPS completed 23 January 2026
Founded2002

What its record can settle

  • what share of workers in a sector, state, metropolitan area, industry, occupation or demographic group were union members or covered by a contract in a year
  • how union density has changed since 1973 (economy-wide) or 1983 (by state)
  • the union wage gap by sector and demographic group

What it cannot settle

  • which unions the members belong to
  • strikes or other labor action
  • what members think of their unions, or any dissent or division inside them

Kept apart

  • NOT official BLS statistics: it is compiled by academics from the CPS using BLS methods
  • NOT reliable for small cells, by its own note
  • NOT a record of labor action or of opinion inside unions
