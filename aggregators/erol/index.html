Encyclopedia of anti-Revisionism On-Line

https://www.marxists.org/history/erol/erol.htm

Movement documentsTexts of the traditionactive
The archive's reading. An organ that keeps a defeated tendency's record in that tendency's own documents, sectioned by its splits and its collapse, so each faction of a split stays stated after the organization that housed it ended. The archive reads its 'Anti-Dogmatists Unable to Unite' and 'Major Issues and Debates' sections as the losing side of the losing side kept for later readers, held by an archive independent of every party it records.
Kindonline encyclopedia of information on, and primary documents from, anti-revisionist movements, organizations and parties, by country
Operatoran EROL Administrator (named only as such on the saved page), within the Marxists Internet Archive's History section by its URL
Unit of recorda primary document of an anti-revisionist movement, organization or party, filed by country and by period subsection; periodicals listed by country
What it recordsthe anti-revisionist movements, organizations and parties of 26 countries, with representative materials from China and Albania; the United States in waves from 1946 and the New Communist Movement to its collapse; Canada 1964 to 1983; the United Kingdom from post-war antecedents to decline and dissolution
Whose record it keepsthe anti-revisionist movements, organizations and parties: their primary documents and periodicals, in the encyclopedia's own periodization
Who holds itan EROL Administrator, under the Marxists Internet Archive (served at www.marxists.org/history/erol/); terms are not stated on the saved EROL pages
The losing sidethe encyclopedia is organized around splits and defeat: U.S. sections titled 'Crises, Splits and More New Parties, 1977-1980', 'Birth of the Anti-Dogmatist Trend, 1976-1977', 'Anti-Dogmatists Unable to Unite, 1978-1980', 'Collapse and Aftermath' and 'Major Issues and Debates'; Canada's 'Crisis and Collapse, 1982-1983'; the UK's 'Ebbing Tide: Decline and Dissolution'. The tendency it records is itself a minority defined against 'revisionism'
Methodcollection and posting of primary documents and information by country and period, with an alphabetical index to the U.S. New Communist Movement subsections (the gathering method is not described on the saved page)
Coveragegeography: 26 country sections (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and 23 others from Australia to Switzerland), counted here from the contents page; representative materials from China and Albania; period: from 1946 (the U.S. First Wave of Anti-Revisionism, 1946-1950) to the 1980s (Canada's Crisis and Collapse, 1982-1983)
Countvalue: 26 country sections (counted here from the contents page); document counts not stated; as_of: 2026-10-31
HoldingsUnited States: three waves of anti-revisionism (1946-1970) and the New Communist Movement from origins (1969) to collapse, with major issues and debates and an alphabetical index; Canada: 1964-1983, with a post-war antecedent (Fergus McKean); United Kingdom: early 1960s to decline and dissolution; 23 further country sections; Anti-Revisionist Periodicals, Magazines and Newspapers by Country
Stated limitsnot stated on the saved pages
Data accessfree public web pages at www.marxists.org/history/erol/
Licencenot stated on the saved EROL pages; the Marxists Internet Archive introduction states that all material within its archives, unless noted otherwise, is public domain
Lifecyclestate: active; detail: served live under marxists.org; the saved contents page carries no date of update; the movements it records ended or dissolved by its own section titles

What its record can settle

  • what an anti-revisionist organization in a covered country stated in its own documents
  • how the encyclopedia periodizes a national movement's waves, splits and collapse
  • which anti-revisionist periodicals it lists by country

What it cannot settle

  • the completeness of its holdings for any organization
  • how its documents were gathered (unchecked here)
  • the record of tendencies outside anti-revisionism

Kept apart

  • NOT an organ of any party it records
  • NOT a general encyclopedia of Marxism: its subject is anti-revisionist movements, organizations and parties
  • NOT a living movement's site: its sections record the movements' collapse and dissolution

Relations

  • part_of: Marxists Internet Archive — EROL is served under www.marxists.org/history/erol/ (the saved source URLs); the saved contents page does not state the relation in prose
