The archive's reading. This holds the organ a defeated critique built for itself: #1725 cites the Reasoner and New Reasoner (1956–59) as the case where the party failed to hold the critics and the critique kept an organ and found a wider field, and here both runs are held whole, ten New Reasoner issues and three of The Reasoner. The holder is a charitable trust with standing independent of the party criticized, and the New Reasoner page states the route ran through the merger into New Left Review.

The losing sidethe New Reasoner page records the case directly: The Reasoner was critical of what its editors saw as the Communist Party of Great Britain's dogmatism; E.P. Thompson and John Saville refused the leadership's instruction to close it, were suspended, resigned in protest at the Soviet invasion of Hungary, and relaunched as the New Reasoner, which ran ten issues and later merged with Universities and Left Review to become New Left Review. The archive holds The Reasoner (3 issues, 1956) and all ten New Reasoner issues

What its record can settle

  • what The Reasoner and New Reasoner printed, issue by issue, after the editors refused the CPGB leadership's order to close
  • where a word or phrase appears across the held runs of British socialist and radical periodicals
  • which issues of each held publication are in the database

What it cannot settle

  • the Communist Party leadership's own account of the 1956 dispute (no party records appear in the saved holdings list)
  • the full runs of publications held only in part where the span exceeds the issues listed (New University: 13 issues, 1960–2024)
  • the terms on which the texts may be reused

Kept apart

  • NOT the Communist Party of Great Britain's archive, though it holds the dissidents' journals and Marxism Today
  • NOT New Left Review: it holds the New Reasoner and Universities & Left Review that merged into it
  • NOT a general library of the left: thirteen periodicals and series
