Marxists Internet Archive

Texts of the traditionArchives and custodyactive
The archive's reading. An independent holder: the Charter keeps the archive politically independent and its sixth tenet, 'We do not judge writers', together with the 2007 end of the Marxist/non-Marxist division, keeps a writer in the record regardless of the verdict any party passed on them. The archive's reading is that this is the holder condition of #1725 without a route: the text stays available to be quoted, and what is kept depends on a volunteer willing to transcribe it.

What its record can settle

  • whether a named author's writing is transcribed in the MIA, and in which language sections
  • how the MIA governs itself and on what terms it holds its texts (Charter, license)
  • the MIA's own scale: documents, data, authors, languages, volunteers, visitors

What it cannot settle

  • whether the MIA's holdings of an author are complete (inclusion depends on a willing volunteer and on intellectual property law)
  • the critical state of a text (the method is transcription; the saved sources state no editorial apparatus)
  • which positions lost within any party (the saved introduction names no such section)

Kept apart

  • NOT a party or tendency organ: its Charter commits it to remaining politically independent
  • NOT an endorsement of the writers it holds
  • NOT a scholarly edition: its texts are volunteer transcriptions
  • NOT complete for any author by its own statement: inclusion depends on a willing volunteer and on intellectual property law
  • NOT limited to Marxist writers since 2007: it indexes Selected Marxist Writers and holds writings relevant to the understanding of Marxism
