Labor Action Tracker

https://striketracker.ilr.cornell.edu/

Labor actionactive
The archive's reading. none stated. The archive reads the tracker as a record of action held by a university outside the unions it counts; it records that a strike happened and keeps nothing of the arguments inside the movement over whether or how to strike.
Kinddatabase (tracker) of strike and labor protest activity across the United States, with a map
OperatorCornell University's ILR School and the University of Illinois' LER School (a collaboration since January 2024); project director Johnnie Kallas, Assistant Professor at Illinois LER
Unit of recorda strike or a labor protest; the map counts locations, so a strike at five locations appears five times
What it recordsstrikes (documented from late 2020) and labor protests (from early 2021) in the United States; a member collects data on strike outcomes since August 2025
Whose record it keepsthe record of strikes and labor protests in the United States, as reported in public sources
Who holds ita university research project (Cornell ILR and Illinois LER), funded at its start by the ILR School and staffed by faculty, graduate and undergraduate research assistants; the spreadsheet version of the data is had by emailing the project director
The losing sidenot recorded
Methodnews-derived event coding: information gathered from public sources and entered by hand, following a methodology document of definitions, protocols and variables (unread here); a Report a Strike link takes submissions
Coveragegeography: United States; period: strikes from late 2020, labor protests from early 2021; the strike database is most accurate as of 2021
Countvalue: not stated in the saved sources; as_of: 2026-10-31

Lifecyclestate: active; detail: originated at the ILR School in late 2020; public on 1 May 2021 as the Cornell ILR Labor Action Tracker; a Cornell ILR and Illinois LER collaboration, rebranded the Labor Action Tracker, since January 2024; current members joined as recently as August 2026
Foundedlate 2020 (public 1 May 2021)

What its record can settle

  • where and when strikes occurred in the United States since 2021, from public reports
  • the general pattern of labor protest activity in the United States since early 2021

What it cannot settle

  • a complete count of labor protests
  • strikes that left no trace in public sources
  • the positions, factions or internal disputes inside the unions and workforces that struck

Kept apart

  • NOT a complete count of labor protests: it aims at a general understanding
  • NOT a count of strikes on its map: the map counts locations
  • NOT official statistics: it is a university project coding public reports
  • NOT a record of dissent inside unions
