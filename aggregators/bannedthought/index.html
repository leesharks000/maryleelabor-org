BannedThought.net

Movement documentsTexts of the traditionactive
The archive's reading. A holder independent of every party whose documents it posts, which keeps the statements of both sides of a split (Nepal's CPN (RM) and CPN (Maoist Centre)) and of several rival CPI (M-L) parties in their own words. Its organizing question is suppression by states, which #1725 names as an outside accelerant; the archive reads its record of internal division as a by-product of that question, kept in the parties' own terms.
Kindweb site that exists 'to struggle against the fascist-like suppression of progressive ideas'; it publicizes suppression and posts documents and publications suppressed elsewhere
Operatornot named in the saved source; the site states it is independent and unaffiliated with any political party (contact freespeech@bannedthought.net)
Unit of recorda posted document or publication: a party document or statement, an issue of a party magazine or bulletin, a pamphlet, filed by country and by party
What it recordsdocuments and publications of revolutionary and Maoist parties and movements, by country (Afghanistan to Yugoslavia), with deep sections on India (CPI (Maoist) and CPI (M-L) parties and their magazines), Nepal (CPN (RM) and CPN (Maoist Centre)) and China (writings of Mao, the Cultural Revolution, the Sino-Soviet 'Great Debate'), plus international associations (RIM, ICL, CCOMPOSA, Comintern) and MLM theory
Whose record it keepsthe documents of parties and movements it judges progressive and suppressed, chiefly Maoist parties, in their own words
Who holds itthe site itself, independent and unaffiliated with any political party by its notice; it is banned in India and some other countries by its own account; the operator, license and terms are not stated on the saved page
The losing sidethe site files rival parties of one tradition side by side: both parties of the Nepal split, the CPN (Revolutionary Maoist) [formerly CPN Maoist] and the CPN (Maoist Centre) [formerly UCPN (Maoist)], each with its documents and statements; several CPI (M-L) parties (the original 1969 party, People's War, Naxalbari, Satyanaran Singh / PCC) and 'Other Parties and Groups Espousing Mao Tsetung Thought'; 'The Views of Various UCPN(M) Leaders'; the Sino-Soviet 'Great Debate'; and a 'Two Lines Struggle Journal'. Its own criterion is suppression by governments, and it names its sections in its own terms ('Revisionist', 'Social-Imperialist')
Methodposting of documents and publications suppressed elsewhere, with reader submissions invited; usually English-language items
Coveragegeography: about fifty country sections and international associations, by the home page's list; period: not stated as a span; sections run from the Chinese Revolution up to 1949 and the early history of the Indian communist movement to contemporary statements; latest dated item on the saved page an addendum of 5 May 2022
Countvalue: not stated in the saved source; as_of: 2026-10-31
Holdingscountry sections from Afghanistan to Yugoslavia and Balkans Region; India: CPI (Maoist) documents and statements, Information Bulletin, People's War and a dozen party magazines; CPI (M-L) parties' documents; Vanguard magazine; Nepal: CPN (RM) and UCPN (M) party documents, the Maoist Information Bulletin, The Worker, The Red Star; China: writings of Mao, magazines, the Cultural Revolution, the Great Debate with the Soviet Union; international: CCOMPOSA, RIM, ICL, Comintern, Red Path, Maoist Road, Two Lines Struggle Journal
Stated limits'because of our own language deficiencies, we are usually only posting items in English'; 'Additional pages for other specific countries will be added as becomes necessary'; its goal is 'simply to expose attempts to censor or suppress progressive ideas and to make available publications and documents which have been suppressed'
Data accessfree public web site; a Google search box for the site; a New Postings list
Licencenot stated in the saved source
Lifecyclestate: active; detail: the home page carries a New Postings list and announces further country pages; the latest date on the saved page is an addendum of 5 May 2022 on its ban in India; the dates of recent postings are unchecked

What its record can settle

  • what a named Maoist party (in India, Nepal and elsewhere) stated in its own documents and magazines, where the site holds them
  • how rival parties of one tradition, including both sides of the Nepal split, stated their positions
  • which governments the site records as suppressing these documents, including its own ban in India

What it cannot settle

  • whether its holdings for any party are complete
  • the provenance and date of each posted document (unchecked here)
  • the documents of tendencies the site does not count as progressive or suppressed

Kept apart

  • NOT affiliated with any political party, by its notice
  • NOT a neutral library of the left: its sympathies are stated, and it posts what it counts as progressive and suppressed
  • NOT multilingual by design: it usually posts items in English
  • NOT a party organ, though most of what it holds is party documents
