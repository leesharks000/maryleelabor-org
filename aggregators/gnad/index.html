Global Nonviolent Action Database

The archive's reading. none stated. The archive reads the case as the unit that carries a campaign's course in prose, which an event count does not; whether a case keeps a defeated tactic or a split inside the campaign is not shown in the saved pages.
Kinddatabase of case studies of nonviolent action campaigns, browsable by case, tag, method and map
OperatorSwarthmore College, including the Peace and Conflict Studies Program, the Peace Collection, and the Lang Center for Civic and Social Responsibility

Whose record it keepsthe record of nonviolent campaigns, written up as cases

What its record can settle

  • how a named nonviolent campaign was conducted, as the case writes it up
  • which campaigns used a given method, through the methods browse

What it cannot settle

  • the frequency of nonviolent action in any country or period (the cases are written up one by one)
  • strikes or campaigns that have no case
  • the positions argued inside a campaign (not shown in the saved pages)

Kept apart

  • NOT a labor dataset: labor cases are one part of a database of nonviolent action of every kind
  • NOT a database of movements: its unit is the campaign
  • NOT an event count
