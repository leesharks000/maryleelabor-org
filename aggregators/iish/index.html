  • whether the IISH holds collections on a named organisation or person in labour and social movements (through its catalogue, unsaved here)
  • which datasets it holds on wages, prices and labour relations

What it cannot settle

  • which collections it holds, from the saved text (the catalogue was not read)
  • whether it keeps the records of minority tendencies, splits or expulsions, from the saved text

Kept apart

  • NOT a party or movement archive: an institute of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences
  • NOT only an archive: also a data centre on socio-economic history and a research institute
  • NOT described in the saved text as keeping any particular tendency's papers
