Meta-aggregationWorker poweractive
The archive's reading. none stated. The operator named it as a possible aggregator source for this page; the archive reads its worker power intersection (organizing, refusal, contestation, enforceable protections) as where it meets the left census. It shares this census's editor, so it is no independent holder in #1725's sense.
Kindthe canonical definition surface for Directionality of Semantic Labor and the interoperable metric program, carrying a meta-aggregator on labor and AI (a census of 15 resources)
OperatorLee Sharks (Semantic Economy Institute / Crimson Hexagonal Archive), the editor of this census
Unit of recordin the meta-aggregator: a resource that observes an intersection of labor and AI, formalized as an entity and tagged by intersection and kind of evidence; on the home page: a DSL reading of a transcript
What it recordsresources that observe the intersections of labor and AI, from data workers' own inquiries and contract inventories to employment trackers and records of how people use AI systems, read for who supplies the labor, who directs it and who receives its gains, and on what evidence
Whose record it keepsthe census's records of 15 labor-and-AI resources (Data Workers' Inquiry, Turkopticon, Fairwork Cloudwork Ratings, Negotiating Tech, the ILO platform policy tracker and others), each with its own unit, operator and evidence kind
Who holds itLee Sharks, under CC BY 4.0; the census is published as data (census.json and questions.json) with every saved source
The losing sidenot recorded: its organizing question is who supplies, directs and receives the gains of labor, and each record states what its resource cannot answer
Methoda census compiled from saved sources, each resource formalized as an entity and read against one question; worked questions name the resources that hold the evidence and what none of them records
Coveragegeography: not stated as a scope; the resources range from global trackers to U.S. contract inventories; period: compiled 2026-10-29
Countvalue: 15 resources across five intersections; as_of: 2026-10-29
Holdings15 resource records across five intersections (labor producing AI, AI governing labor, labor performed with AI, gains and losses, worker power) and twelve kinds of evidence; worked questions with the resources that answer them and what none of them records; the DSL metric and its interoperable program on the home page
Stated limits'A record states only what its resource measures or reports.'; 'No record in the census measures the distribution of productivity gains between workers, employers, platforms and model providers.'
Data accesspublic pages; the census as data (census.json, questions.json) with saved sources

Founded2026 (DSL defined by Lee Sharks)

What its record can settle

  • which labor-and-AI resources hold which kind of evidence (exposure estimate, employment outcome, worker testimony, negotiated provision and the rest)
  • which resources record organizing and negotiated protections (its worker power intersection)

What it cannot settle

  • how a left organization holds or loses internal critique
  • the record of left movements, parties or strikes outside labor and AI

Kept apart

  • NOT labor economics: its page reads 'DSL measures semantic, not wage, labor'
  • NOT a census of left politics: its census covers labor and AI
  • NOT independent of this census: it has the same editor
