The archive reads the tag index as holding writers of anti-party and left-communist tendencies next to strike histories, which would make it an organ for positions the parties lost; the saved text does not state that as its purpose, and the reading waits on its About page.

Unit of recorda page (a text, article, biography or gallery) filed under one or more tags; the saved index lists tags
What it recordsas the saved tag list shows: strikes (the 1926 general strike, the 1934 Minneapolis Teamsters strike, the Flint sit-down strike), movements and organisations (Abahlali baseMjondolo, Anti Fascist Action, the CNT, the civil rights movement, France 1968), and writers (Amadeo Bordiga, Anton Pannekoek, Antonio Negri, CLR James, Emma Goldman, Gilles Dauvé, Guy Debord); related-content blocks on the 404 pages show a Solidarity (London) pamphlet by Cornelius Castoriadis and an article edited from News and Letters
Whose record it keepswritings and accounts of anarchism, strikes and social movements, as the tag list names them; whose record beyond the tag list is not stated in the saved text
Who holds itnot stated in the saved text: the About pages answered 404; the index carries Donate and Help out links and a user account (Log in / Register)

Methodnot stated in the saved text; pages are submitted by site accounts ('Submitted by libcom on October 14, 2006') and carry user comments

What its record can settle

  • which subjects, strikes, organisations and writers the collections are tagged under (as far as the saved index runs)

What it cannot settle

  • who runs the site and on what terms (the About pages were unread)
  • how many texts the collections hold
  • how texts are selected, transcribed or edited
  • the licence of the texts

Kept apart

  • NOT a library in the saved text's own words: the address /library redirects to /collections
  • NOT described in the saved sources as to operator, method or licence
  • NOT a dataset of strikes: strikes appear as tags on texts
